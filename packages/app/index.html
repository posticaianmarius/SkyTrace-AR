Accuracy eval

Tap exactly where a labelled object really is in the camera image. Each tap logs the error between our label and the real position.

Calibrate

Tap exactly on a real object you can see: the Moon, a bright planet, or a labelled plane. Never look at the Sun; aim the camera only. Two taps on objects far apart also fix the lens field of view.

SkyTrace AR
